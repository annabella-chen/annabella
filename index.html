WEEK 05

Data Visualisation with Excel

05 ♡

The main challenge I found in this task was how to turn abstract policy texts into consistent numerical codes. It was hard to stay objective when judging how clear each university’s AI rule was. There was also sampling bias, because we only selected information from official university websites, which left out the voices of students and teaching staff.

I hoped to learn the differences between universities’ generative AI assessment policies from our dataset. We successfully sorted out and coded these policy documents in Excel and created variables to compare policy clarity. However, our results can only reflect the written rules, not the real experience of users, so we were only partially successful.

If I had more time and resources, I would add Phase 2 and collect student perspectives, including student experiences and feedback. Combining student interviews or surveys with the existing policy data could help create a more complete picture of how these policies work in practice.
